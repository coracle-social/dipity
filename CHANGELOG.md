# Changelog

# 0.1.4

* Add a contact by pressing a button on the people page, or on the board when it is empty; the other phone shows the request only when you press it
* Pairing no longer pops up on its own, and anybody who comes into range while you wait is asked too
* The people page lists only the people you have named, with a count of phones in range above them
* Pictures now arrive when the faster transfer between two phones fails to open, and after the two phones first pair
* The count of phones in range is right after reopening the app on iPhone

# 0.1.3

* Both phones now show the same pairing digits when they find each other at the same moment
* Pairing digits are bolder and colored by digit, so they're easier to compare
* Report a flagged post to the people who make Dipity by email
* Agree to the terms of use when you first open the app
* Clearer wording for muting, blocking and suppressing someone from a flagged post

# 0.1.2

* Picture posts, and topics with a filter and muted topics on the board
* Flag posts; what trusted contacts flag is warned about or hidden
* Mute, block and suppress people separately
* Meet anyone nearby while the app is open, with background discovery as a setting
* One sharing setting: contacts, network or anyone
* Posts go to the trash and can be restored, and deleting your own post retracts it
* Pair by comparing six digits
* Log out, and more reliable sync between iPhones and Android phones

* First release
